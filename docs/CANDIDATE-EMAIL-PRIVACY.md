# Candidate email identity

Automatic receipts, automated screening rejections and reminders use `no-reply@aicaseleads.com` for From and Reply-To. Job teams control internal recipients only. Queued legacy sender/reply headers are discarded. Templates containing any current or former staff or connected-account email fail closed for correction; the worker never silently sends those addresses to an applicant.

Connect a dedicated authorized no-reply mailbox, or configure that address as an accepted Gmail send-as identity on the connected account. The alias path requires reconnecting Workspace with `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.settings.basic`; the worker verifies `GET https://gmail.googleapis.com/gmail/v1/users/me/settings/sendAs` has a matching `sendAsEmail` and `verificationStatus: accepted` before each send. Missing aliases, pending verification, and missing permission block delivery and surface a failed integration task. Internal team alerts and staff invitations use the configured recruiter Gmail account, never the company sales connection.

The application controls the MIME From/Reply-To and never adds applicant Cc, Bcc or Sender headers. Google controls transport headers. Before claiming staff identities hidden, inspect an actually received message's From, Reply-To, Sender, Return-Path, expanded sender details and body. A dedicated no-reply account is necessary if Google reveals the underlying account through an alias. Do not claim this changes earlier delivered messages.

Calendar/Meet invitations are a separate Google product. The organizer address can remain visible to interview attendees. A shared recruiting calendar/account is required if personal organizer identities must also be hidden.

## Replies and owner notifications

The no-reply address must reject inbound messages, not forward them to the connected account. Configure a Google Workspace incoming-mail rule matching only the exact no-reply recipient, with Reject Message. This is separate from outbound Gmail send-as verification. Prove it with a controlled inbound delivery attempt and mail-log evidence before claiming it works. Do not recommend replying to no-reply for privacy requests. Applicant help and privacy requests go to `/help` and the private recruiting dashboard, without staff email notifications.

Every automatic no-reply message includes a do-not-reply notice and the help URL. Recruiter-initiated messages instead explain that replies reach the recruiter. Existing delivered messages cannot be edited.

Ownership and Google authorization do not automatically subscribe the owner to job alerts. To stop owner notifications, remove the owner from each job's team, from overdue-review `fallbackAdmins`, and from legacy applicant assignments where no explicit job-team configuration exists. Current routing is checked again before queued messages are sent. Keep Calendar and Drive credentials intact. Calendar-organizer messages from Google are separate from app notification settings.

## Updated recruiter reply policy

Automatic receipts, automated screening rejections and timed reminders use no-reply Reply-To; inbound replies are rejected. Recruiter-initiated messages, manual decisions and assessment/interview invitations record the authenticated actor and direct Reply-To only to that active recruiter. Sales is never a permitted reply contact. If the actor is unavailable, only the explicit active job reply contact or active application assignee may be used; otherwise sending stops for setup. No applicant Cc/Bcc is added. Recruiter addresses are intentionally visible for these correspondence messages, and replies reach their own inbox, not automatically the hiring dashboard.

Set `CANDIDATE_MAIL_ACCOUNT=mariam@aicaseleads.com` only as the intended authorized account. Candidate mail checks Google's actual profile against this setting and refuses sales transport, even if sales has a no-reply alias. Setting the variable does not authorize Maria's Gmail. Her personal Calendar grant is insufficient. Maria must connect her separate recruiting Gmail under My setup and approve the requested mail permissions. Keep existing company Drive/Calendar authorization intact. Candidate mail remains queued until her personal Gmail grant and verified no-reply alias are ready. Recruiter-initiated messages use the authenticated recruiter’s dedicated Gmail account and From address; the actor must connect their own recruiting Gmail. Do not tell users this is activated merely because the setting exists.

Company Google credentials now serve Drive and Calendar only. Internal notifications, staff invitations, inbound hiring-mail synchronization and uncertain-send reconciliation use the configured recruiting Gmail grant. Missing recruiter authorization leaves mail queued or failed for setup, while Drive archival continues. Replies for other individual recruiter accounts remain in those inboxes unless separately synchronized; do not claim every recruiter inbox is imported.
